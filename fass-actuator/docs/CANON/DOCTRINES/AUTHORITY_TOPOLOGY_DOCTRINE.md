# Canon Doctrine — Authority Topology

## AT-1 — Authority Composition
Authority shall not be centralized, duplicated, or inferred.
Authority exists only within explicitly declared envelopes,
composed hierarchically and bound to Canon.

---

## AT-2 — Envelope Isolation
No Authority Envelope may assume authority from another
except through an explicit parent authority chain.

---

## AT-3 — Witness Supremacy
Witness records generated under Canon supersede logs,
testimony, and system assertions in all determinations of execution truth.

---

## AT-4 — Exposure Invariance
Making authority visible does not increase risk.
Hidden authority is unbounded risk.
