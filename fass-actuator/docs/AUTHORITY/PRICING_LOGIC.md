# FASS Envelope Pricing Logic

## Core Principle

FASS pricing scales with risk absorbed, not control granted.

---

## Pricing Axes

### Axis 1 — Envelope Role
- Shadow (Witness-only)
- Operational (Enforcement)
- Root (Cross-envelope authority)

### Axis 2 — Envelope Scope
- Single system
- Multi-system
- Cross-domain
- Cross-jurisdiction

### Axis 3 — Envelope Exposure
- Internal
- Auditor-visible
- Regulator-visible

---

## What FASS Does Not Charge For

- Users
- API calls
- Log volume
- Compute usage

---

## Why This Is Fair

The more liability FASS absorbs, the more value it provides.
The customer pays for reduced uncertainty and provable compliance.
