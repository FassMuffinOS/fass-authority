# Authority Envelope Model

## Definition
An Authority Envelope is the atomic unit of authority in FASS.

Each envelope is:
- Canon-bound
- Scope-limited
- Independently auditable
- Explicitly revocable

---

## Envelope Types

### 1. Root Authority Envelope
- Organization-wide governance
- Policy ownership
- Canon reference point

### 2. Operational Envelopes
- Perform execution
- Enforce allow/deny decisions
- Limited to declared scope

### 3. Shadow Envelopes
- Witness-only
- Read-only
- Auditor and regulator visibility

---

## Composition Rule

Envelopes compose hierarchically.
They do not merge, infer, or inherit authority implicitly.

---

## Risk Model

More envelopes do not increase risk.
They expose and contain existing risk.

Hidden authority is unbounded risk.
