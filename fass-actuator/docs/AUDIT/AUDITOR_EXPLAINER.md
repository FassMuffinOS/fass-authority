# FASS Auditor Explainer

## What FASS Is

FASS is a computational authority verification system that produces continuous, cryptographically witnessed evidence of execution and policy enforcement.

It does not interpret law or replace auditors.

---

## What Changes for Auditors

Traditional audits rely on sampling and reconstruction.
FASS provides complete, witnessed execution records.

---

## Shadow Envelopes

- Read-only
- Non-executable
- Non-modifiable
- Continuous visibility

---

## Witness Supremacy

Witness records supersede logs and testimony.
If it occurred, it is provable.
If it did not occur, it cannot be fabricated.

---

## Audit Outcome

Audits become verification, not investigation.

FASS does not make systems compliant.
It makes non-compliance undeniable.
