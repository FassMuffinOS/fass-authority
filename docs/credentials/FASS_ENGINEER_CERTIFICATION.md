# FASS Engineer Certification (FEC)

**Credential:** FASS Engineer Certification  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Audience:** Systems Engineers, AI Engineers, Infrastructure Architects  

---

## 1. Purpose

The FASS Engineer Certification defines the minimum competency required to
design, build, or modify authority-bound systems.

This certification exists to prevent systemic failure caused by engineers who
confuse computation, inference, or optimization with authority.

---

## 2. Core Principle

A FASS-certified engineer understands that:

> Systems do not misbehave.
> They only execute the logic they were designed with,
> including the assumptions they inherit.

There is no concept of emergent authority.

---

## 3. Required Knowledge Domains

A candidate MUST demonstrate mastery of:

- Authority vs inference
- Entropy and recursive verification
- Structural constraints and invariants
- Unrepresentable invalid states
- Witnessed execution
- Design-time safety guarantees

---

## 4. Prohibited Mental Models

A candidate FAILS certification if they:

- Propose runtime safety checks as sufficient
- Rely on probabilistic alignment
- Attribute failures to emergent behavior
- Assume models can self-govern authority
- Depend on post-hoc correction mechanisms

These models are incompatible with FASS.

---

## 5. Certification Examination Criteria

A candidate MUST be able to:

1. Prove why recursive authority verification diverges
2. Design a system where unauthorized states cannot exist
3. Explain how entropy is bounded structurally
4. Identify flawed assumptions in a failed system
5. Map authority constraints across software and hardware

Answers must be structural, not behavioral.

---

## 6. Hiring Bar Definition

A FASS-certified engineer:

- Designs systems that cannot misbehave
- Eliminates entire classes of runtime checks
- Reduces compute by reducing uncertainty
- Treats safety as a construction problem

A non-certified engineer is restricted from modifying authority boundaries.

---

## 7. Certification Outcome

Certification grants permission to:

- Define authority boundaries
- Modify execution admissibility
- Architect FASS-compliant systems

Certification does NOT grant permission to override authority.

---

## 8. Revocation

Certification SHALL be revoked if an engineer:

- Introduces recursive authority checks
- Allows runtime authority mutation
- Treats safety as probabilistic
- Violates FASS-001 principles

---

## 9. Summary

FASS engineers do not build systems that hope to be safe.

They build systems that cannot be unsafe.
